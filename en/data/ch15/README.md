# Chapter 15 data

Exercise data for Python for Business Analytics by Daniel de Abreu Pereira Uhr. Business datasets are fictional and author-created. Revenue and revenue targets represent U.S. dollars in ordinary currency units, not thousands. CSV margin is a percentage (28.4 means 28.4%); target_margin_fraction in Excel is a fraction (0.29 means 29%). The CSV is an intentionally retained legacy export using UTF-8, semicolons, and decimal commas. This is a property of this file, not a claim about standard U.S. formatting. Excel sheets: units/targets. Ten branch–month records; March 2026 contains only North/South. Dates, numeric values and missing coverage match the author's teaching data; no exchange-rate conversion.

Census: U.S. Census Bureau, TIGERweb, States layer, January 1, 2026 vintage. Source: https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TIGERweb/State_County/MapServer/0 . Acquired October 27, 2026. Query endpoint ends /query; where=REGION IN ('1','2','3','4'); outFields=STATE,STUSAB,NAME,REGION,DIVISION; returnGeometry=false; orderByFields=STATE; resultRecordCount=100; f=json. Fifty states plus DC, without territories or geometry. FIPS codes remain two-character strings. Raw JSON retained without change.

Federal Reserve: Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, G.17. Source: https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g17/related_data/US3_IP.csv ; acquired October 27, 2026. Methodology: https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g17/About.htm . Raw CSV retained without change. Select AIP_IX, total industrial production, not seasonally adjusted, index 2017=100, January 2023–December 2024. fed_ip_2023_2024_snapshot.csv reshapes those 24 months to rows; dates are ISO first-of-month labels and values retain the original four decimals. Coverage is manufacturing, mining, and electric/gas utilities; the index is not a dollar amount, GDP, or a measure directly comparable in level to Brazil's IBC-Br.
