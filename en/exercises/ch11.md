## Chapter Supplement

The supplement contains 21 exercises: concatenation and keys (01–07), merging datasets (08–14), and the Nexus application (15–21). Work in order within each block. Building the data is already part of Exercises 02, 08, and 16; no additional preparation is required. Open tasks in blocks A and B allow different values and registries. Block C supplies fixed data. Monetary values are in U.S. dollars and quantities in units.

Work through and justify your answers before consulting the solutions in the GitHub worked solutions. Check the observational unit, keys, rows, and matches after each integration.

| P1       | C1          |   800 |
| P2       | C2          |  1200 |

| customer_id | customer | segment  |

| C1          | Aurora   | Business |
