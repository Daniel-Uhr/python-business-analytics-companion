## Chapter Supplement

The supplement contains 21 exercises: dates and validation (01–07), periods and indicators (08–14), and the Beacon application (15–21). Each block starts with its own dataset; work in order within the block. Data construction is part of Exercises 01, 08, and 16, with no additional setup. In block A, the solution’s value column is illustrative, optional, and has no specified monetary unit. Revenue in blocks B/C is in U.S. dollars; service interactions are counts. Dates use month/day/year with an explicit format.

Work through and justify your answers before consulting the solutions in the GitHub worked solutions. Keep invalid dates unresolved and compare period coverage before interpreting rates.

Supplementary materials: [student notebook](../ch12-student.ipynb).
