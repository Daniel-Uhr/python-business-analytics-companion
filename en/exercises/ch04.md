Use `margin = 28`, `margin_target = 30`, `revenue = 210000`, and `revenue_target = 200000`. Margins are percentages and revenue amounts are in U.S. dollars. Evaluate an expression with `and`, another with `or`, and negate a comparison of your choice with `not`. Explain each answer.

### Exercise 03 — Application: a delinquency threshold

### Exercise 05 — Application: eligibility with two criteria

A proposal is eligible when `return_value >= 12` and `risk <= 6`. Apply the rule to a return value of 14 and risk of 5. Use the exercise’s supplied scales; do not add an unspecified unit or financial interpretation.

### Case: Prism Customer Support Center

The fictional U.S. center tracks ten waiting times in minutes: `wait_times = [4, 12, 7, 3, 15, 8, 5, 11, 6, 2]`. The target is service within seven minutes. Bands are: up to five minutes, fast; over five and up to seven, within target and over five minutes; over seven, above target. Fast interactions also meet the overall target but belong to a separate band.

How can we distribute interactions across these bands and identify particularly long waits? Work with the ten records in their supplied order without changing the data.

<a id="ex-logica-repeticao-c1"></a>

### Exercise 13 — Prism: classifying three bands

Use the case data and traverse all waiting times. Classify each interaction as fast, within target but over 5 minutes, or above target. Use `if`, `elif`, and `else`. Explain the order of conditions. Present classifications only here; counters will be built in the next exercise.

<a id="ex-logica-repeticao-c2"></a>

### Exercise 14 — Prism: counters and an accumulator

Process the same data again with `fast_count`, `within_target_band_count`, `above_target_count`, and `total` initialized to zero. Count each band and accumulate all waiting times in the same traversal. `within_target_band_count` represents only the band above 5 and up to 7 minutes. Preserve the results for Exercise 15.

<a id="ex-logica-repeticao-c3"></a>

### Exercise 15 — Prism: waiting-time metrics

Using the results of Exercise 14, calculate the total interaction count, overall mean waiting time in minutes, and percentage above target. Format the mean and percentage to two decimal places.

<a id="ex-logica-repeticao-c4"></a>

### Exercise 16 — Prism: particularly long waits

Treat waits as particularly long when they simultaneously exceed the seven-minute target and last at least ten minutes. Express the condition with `and`, implement a counter for this subset, and explain both thresholds.

<a id="ex-logica-repeticao-c5"></a>

### Exercise 17 — Prism: an f-string report

Use the results of Exercises 14–16 to produce an f-string report containing the total, count in each band, mean, percentage above target, and count of particularly long waits. Identify the mean’s unit and label the intermediate band “Within target, over 5 minutes.”

<a id="ex-logica-repeticao-c6"></a>

### Exercise 18 — Prism: interpreting the results

Write three to five sentences interpreting the distribution across bands, the mean, the percentage above target, and the subset of particularly long waits. Do not make causal claims or classify the center’s overall performance without an external benchmark.
