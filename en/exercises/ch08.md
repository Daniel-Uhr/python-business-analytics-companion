## Chapter Supplement

The supplement contains 19 exercises in three blocks: selection and conditions (01–07), transformations (08–13), and the Vertex application (14–19). Each block begins with a dataset preparation that you must also program. The values are author-created illustrations; no external files are needed. Attempt the tasks before consulting the solutions chapter in the GitHub worked solutions.

Work in order within each block. In Block B, the balance from 08 is reused in 12 and remains in the dataset for intermediate exercises; 13 selects the five original columns to build its own sequence. In Block C, the measures from 14 must exist before the copy in 17; the report from 18 and the selection from 15 are reused in 19. Do not reuse the copy in thousands from 09 for calculations with values in dollars. Record the requested interpretations and explanations.
