## Exercises — Block C

<a id="case-nexus-projects"></a>

### Case: Nexus Projects

The fictional U.S. company Nexus tracks Expansion, Automation, Logistics, and Digitalization, in that order. Their budgets are USD 320,000, USD 185,000, USD 240,000, and USD 150,000, respectively. The portfolio uses the statuses Planned, In progress, and Completed. The reference period is the third quarter of 2026.

How can we organize and retrieve this information using a suitable structure for each need? The supplied data do not assign individual statuses, owners, or durations to projects; where requested, these attributes will receive explicit illustrative choices.

<a id="ex-estruturas-dados-c1"></a>

### Exercise 13 — Organizing projects and budgets

Use the Nexus case data to create lists of projects and budgets in the same order. Access the first and last projects and use slicing to select the two middle projects.

<a id="ex-estruturas-dados-c2"></a>

### Exercise 14 — Summarizing budgets
