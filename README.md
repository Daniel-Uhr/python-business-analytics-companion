# Exercícios e resoluções · Exercises and worked solutions

Materiais suplementares de **Python para Business Analytics** e **Python for Business Analytics**, de Daniel de Abreu Pereira Uhr.

- [Português — exercícios e resoluções por capítulo](pt-br/README.md)
- [English — exercises and worked solutions by chapter](en/README.md)

Este repositório oferece somente os enunciados, as resoluções comentadas, os notebooks do estudante e os dados, figuras e orientações necessários às atividades. Os 16 capítulos de exposição, seus exemplos explicados e os notebooks de exposição (companion) ficam reservados às obras comerciais e não são distribuídos aqui.

This repository provides only exercise statements, worked solutions, student notebooks, and the data, figures, and instructions needed for the activities. The 16 teaching chapters, their explained examples, and the teaching companion notebooks are reserved for the commercial books and are not distributed here.

Leia os enunciados, registre sua tentativa e depois consulte as resoluções. Read the exercises, record your own attempt, and then consult the solutions. Download the entire package to preserve the data folder structure.

English fictional scenarios use U.S. company names and U.S. dollars. Chapter 15 uses actual U.S. official data in English and Brazilian official data in Portuguese; results are specific to their sources.

Materials were prepared from the author's teaching course, with bilingual adaptation using AI tools and author review. The exercise code, results, and data were checked in the recorded environments. Fictional scenarios do not represent actual businesses.
