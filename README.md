# Exercises and Worked Solutions

Supplementary materials for **Python para Business Analytics** (Portuguese edition)
and **Python for Business Analytics** (English edition), by Daniel de Abreu Pereira Uhr.

## Choose your language

- **Português:** [exercícios e resoluções por capítulo](pt-br/README.md)
- **English:** [exercises and worked solutions by chapter](en/README.md)

## What this repository contains

This repository provides only exercise statements, worked solutions, student notebooks,
and the data, figures, and instructions needed for the activities. The 16 teaching
chapters, their explained examples, and the teaching companion notebooks are reserved
for the books and are not distributed here.

## How to use these materials

Read the exercises, record your own attempt, and then consult the worked solutions.
Download the entire package to preserve the folder structure of the data.

Each language folder has its own business scenarios: the Portuguese materials use
Brazilian settings and Brazilian reais, and the English materials use U.S. company
names and U.S. dollars. Chapter 15 uses official data from Brazilian sources in
Portuguese and from U.S. sources in English; results are specific to each source.
Fictional scenarios do not represent actual businesses.
